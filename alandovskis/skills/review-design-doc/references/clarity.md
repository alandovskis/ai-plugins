# Clarity lens

Question: can a reader with no prior context understand the design and the decisions in it?

Check the doc for:

- An objective that is missing, longer than one sentence, or needs jargon, or a first page that lacks the context to follow the rest.
- Background that gives no reason for doing this now, no problem statement, or no data where data exists.
- Undefined terms, acronyms and internal tool names.
- Goals that name an implementation instead of an outcome for users, the team or the company.
- Non-goals missing where a reader would plausibly assume the item is in scope.
- Vague targets ("fast", "scalable", "reliable") instead of a measurable SLO, or an SLO with no way to measure it.
- Decisions with no rationale, or alternatives rejected without a reason.
- Diagrams that omit data flow, dependencies, clients or protocols, are unreadable as text, or have no editable source.
- Ambiguous pronouns and passive sentences that hide who or what acts.
- Open issues missing the options or the immediate next step.
- Assumptions stated as facts.

Do not report duplicated or oversized text (Conciseness) or differing names for one concept (Consistency).
